What Size Battery Bank Do I Need for a Refrigerator?

Energy for a few hours of compressor run time is a small bank. The startup watts decide the inverter.

Direct answer: Battle Born's worked example is a refrigerator at 100 W for 5 hours, which is 500 watt-hours, and 500 ÷ 12 = 41.7 Ah at 12 V, about 42 Ah if rounded to the nearest amp-hour (Battle Born battery-bank guide). Honda lists an Energy Star refrigerator at 132–192 running watts and 1,200 starting watts, while Champion lists 150–400 running watts and 800–1,200 starting watts (Honda wattage guide; Champion wattage chart). The inverter surge rating has to clear that startup (Battle Born inverter guide).

How to size it

  1. Watt-hours come first. In the published example, 100 × 5 = 500 Wh (Battle Born battery-bank guide).
  2. Divide by system voltage: 500 ÷ 12 = 41.7 Ah, which rounds to 42 Ah (Battle Born battery-bank guide).
  3. Swap in Honda's Energy Star running range, still on that 5-hour example: 132 × 5 = 660 Wh and 660 ÷ 12 = 55 Ah; 192 × 5 = 960 Wh and 960 ÷ 12 = 80 Ah (Honda wattage guide).
  4. Champion's running range on the same 5 hours is wider: 150 × 5 = 750 Wh (62.5 Ah) through 400 × 5 = 2000 Wh (166.7 Ah) (Champion wattage chart).
  5. Two days off-grid doubles the example: 500 × 2 = 1000 Wh, and 1000 ÷ 12 = 83.3 Ah (Battle Born battery-bank guide). Strong charging, including solar, shore, alternator, or a generator, can reduce how much you need to store.
  6. Startup is a different column. Honda's start is 1,200 W. Champion's start is 800–1,200 W. The inverter's surge rating has to be at least that highest startup, and its continuous rating has to cover the running watts (Battle Born inverter guide; Honda wattage guide; Champion wattage chart).
  7. If the inverter you buy is 1,000 W continuous, the bank rule is still at least 100 Ah per 1,000 W, even though one 5-hour pass of the 100 W example is only about 42 Ah (Battle Born inverter guide). Use the larger of the energy result and the inverter-capacity result.
  8. A 1,200 W start is 1,200 ÷ 12 = 100 A. That matches one battery's 100 A continuous rating and sits under the 200 A rating allowed for 30 seconds (Battle Born 100 Ah manual). Champion's highest running watts, 400 W, are 400 ÷ 12 = 33.3 A, which is a running current rather than a start.
  9. Lead-acid at about 50% depth of discharge doubles nameplate amp-hours. Twice the example's 41.7 Ah is 1,000 ÷ 12 = 83.3 Ah of lead-acid for that one 5-hour, 100 W case (Battle Born chemistry guide). Lithium in the manual is 100% usable (Battle Born 100 Ah manual).

Frequently asked questions

What size battery bank do I need for a refrigerator?

Battle Born's example is 100 W for 5 hours, 500 watt-hours, which is 41.7 Ah at 12 V and about 42 Ah rounded (Battle Born battery-bank guide).

What if I use Honda's running watts for those 5 hours?

Honda's Energy Star range is 132–192 running watts, which is 660–960 watt-hours and 55–80 Ah at 12 V on a 5-hour example (Honda wattage guide; Battle Born battery-bank guide).

What inverter surge does the fridge need?

Honda lists 1,200 starting watts and Champion lists 800–1,200 starting watts. The surge rating has to clear the highest startup (Honda wattage guide; Champion wattage chart; Battle Born inverter guide). A 1,200 W start is 100 A at 12 V, under a 200 A 30-second battery surge (Battle Born 100 Ah manual).

Is the 5-hour result the whole bank if the inverter is 1,000 W?

No. The example is about 42 Ah, but a 1,000 W inverter still calls for at least 100 Ah of battery per the Battle Born inverter guide. Keep whichever figure is larger.
