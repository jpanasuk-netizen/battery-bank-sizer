What Size Battery for a Trolling Motor and Fish Finder?

One 12 V battery, two loads. No brand pick. The motor rows are Minn Kota's and the fish finder rows are Garmin's.

Direct answer: To size a battery for a trolling motor and fish finder, add both draws. Garmin's ECHOMAP UHD manual lists typical 12 V draw of 0.7 A (6-inch), 0.8 A (7-inch) and 1 A (9-inch). Minn Kota lists maximum draw of 30 A for a 30 lb motor and 50 A for 50 or 55 lb, peaks that occur only intermittently.

Sources: Garmin ECHOMAP UHD Owner's Manual, Specifications, and Minn Kota battery and wiring charts Rev. 8.17.2021, both read November 24, 2026. Every amp-hour figure below is arithmetic on this page.

Shared-battery amp-hour builder

Pick the motor thrust and the fish finder screen size, enter how many hours you are on the water and how much of that time the motor is at full throttle. The builder multiplies Minn Kota's maximum amp draw by the full-throttle hours and Garmin's typical draw by all the hours. It is an estimate built on maximum draw: Minn Kota does not publish a part-throttle draw, so time below full throttle is not counted, and 100% is the worst case.

One battery or two?

Next to a motor's 30 to 50 A, the fish finder's 0.7 to 1 A is small, so on one battery it adds little to the amp-hours. Minn Kota's own chart recommends a separate deep cycle battery for the trolling motor when a crank battery starts a gasoline outboard. Adding a second battery? Limits

The motor figure is Minn Kota's maximum, which it says should not be used as a continuous load rating; part-throttle draw is not published in the chart. The fish finder figure is Garmin's typical draw for the ECHOMAP UHD only; other units differ, so check your own manual. This page does not apply a depth-of-discharge factor.
