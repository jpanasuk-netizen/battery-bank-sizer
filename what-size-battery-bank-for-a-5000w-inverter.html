What Size Battery Bank Do I Need for a 5000W Inverter?

Five 100 Ah batteries are the 12 V match for 5,000 W, and this inverter sits above the published surge-limiter line.

Direct answer: A 5,000 W inverter needs at least 500 Ah at 12 V, five 100 Ah batteries, from the rule of 100 amp-hours per 1,000 watts (Battle Born inverter guide). Full load is 5,000 ÷ 12 = 416.7 A, so five batteries in parallel keep each near 83.3 A, under 100 A continuous (Battle Born 100 Ah manual). Around 3,500 W and above, a current surge limiter may be needed, and 5,000 W is above that line (Battle Born inverter guide).

How to size it

  1. Multiply the rule through: 5,000 ÷ 1,000 × 100 = 500 Ah, and 500 ÷ 100 = 5 batteries of 100 Ah (Battle Born inverter guide; Battle Born battery-bank guide).
  2. Current at 12 V is 5,000 ÷ 12 = 416.7 A. Five batteries rated 100 A continuous each provide 500 A continuous, and each battery then carries 5,000 ÷ 12 ÷ 5 = 83.3 A (Battle Born 100 Ah manual).
  3. The 30-second surge rating stacks the same way: 200 A × 5 = 1000 A, which covers a 416.7 A running draw. The feature line says a 1/2-second surge for loads over 200 A, and the spec table lists a 0.5-second surge for loads over 500 A. A sustained 416.7 A draw is not that short surge, so this design stays inside the 100 A continuous rating per battery (Battle Born 100 Ah manual).
  4. Two batteries are the published floor for larger inverters (Battle Born inverter guide). Two batteries are 200 A continuous, and 416.7 A is more than twice that, so two batteries fail the current test even though they satisfy the "minimum of two" sentence.
  5. Because 5,000 W is above about 3,500 W, plan on a current surge limiter (Battle Born inverter guide). The same paragraph says to size slightly above the calculated load for losses and expansion and does not give a percent, so none is added here.
  6. Lead-acid at about 50% depth of discharge makes the 500 Ah lithium rule a 1000 Ah lead-acid nameplate (Battle Born chemistry guide). Lithium in the manual is listed at 100% usable depth of discharge (Battle Born 100 Ah manual).
  7. 24 V halves the current: 5,000 ÷ 24 = 208.3 A instead of 416.7 A. 208.3 A is more than 200 A, so three batteries at 100 A continuous are still required in parallel. The 500 Ah figure is the published 12 V capacity rule and is not rewritten into a different amp-hour target for 24 V (Battle Born inverter guide; Battle Born 100 Ah manual).
  8. Charge current scales per battery. The manual's max is 50 A, and five batteries can accept 50 × 5 = 250 A (Battle Born 100 Ah manual). That much charge is a panel problem: panels to charge a battery.

Frequently asked questions

What size battery bank do I need for a 5000W inverter?

At least 500 amp-hours at 12 volts, five 100 Ah batteries. The Battle Born inverter guide uses 100 amp-hours per 1,000 watts, and 5,000 ÷ 1,000 × 100 = 500.

How many parallel batteries does 5000W need at 12 volts?

Five. 5,000 ÷ 12 = 416.7 amps, and five batteries at 100 amps continuous each leave about 83.3 amps on a battery (Battle Born 100 Ah manual).

Does a 5000W inverter need a surge limiter?

Plan for one. The Battle Born inverter guide says a current surge limiter may be needed around 3,500 watts and above, and 5,000 W is above that line.

What changes at 24 volts?

Current drops to 208.3 amps (5,000 ÷ 24), from 416.7 amps at 12 volts. 208.3 amps is still over 200 amps, so three batteries in parallel are needed to stay under 100 amps each (Battle Born 100 Ah manual). The 500 Ah rule is not restated as a different number (Battle Born inverter guide).
