What size generator do you need? The 60-second answer, then the details.

Quick answer: add up the RUNNING watts of everything you'll power at once, add the LARGEST starting-watt item (motors take 2–3× their running wattage at startup), and buy a generator rated ~20% above that total. A typical off-grid cabin needs 3,000–4,500 running watts; a home-backup essentials setup needs 5,000–7,500.

Battery bank instead of fuel? (The 2026 question)

If your daily load is under ~5 kWh and you have solar, a LiFePO4 battery bank often beats a generator: no fuel storage, no exhaust, silent, 10-year lifespan. See our battery bank sizing tables — the crossover point is roughly $4,600 in batteries vs a $1,200 generator plus $80/month of fuel. Hybrid setups (small generator + battery bank) beat both for full off-grid.
