Frequently asked questions

What size generator do I need for a house?

Add the running watts of the loads you will power at once, then the largest starting watts. This page does not publish a house wattage total.

What size generator do I need for an off-grid cabin?

Add the cabin's running watts and its largest starting watts. This page does not publish a cabin wattage total. Battery-bank sizing for a cabin is on the cabin page.

What size generator for a 2000 sq ft house?

Floor area is not a wattage. Add the running watts of the loads in that house and the largest starting watts. This page does not publish a wattage for a 2,000 sq ft house.

How many watts does a refrigerator use on a generator?

This page does not publish a refrigerator wattage. The refrigerator battery page is the battery-bank side of that load.

Is a battery bank better than a generator?

Compare a battery bank with a generator only after you have the daily watt-hours and the seller's prices. This page does not publish those prices.
