What Size Battery Bank Do I Need for a CPAP?

A CPAP is a small, steady load. The bank is set by hours asleep, not by inverter surge.

Direct answer: Champion lists a CPAP at 30–60 W, and a heated CPAP at 60–120 W, with no separate starting watts (Champion wattage chart). Using 8 hours as the example night, that is 240–960 watt-hours, or 20–80 Ah at 12 V (Battle Born battery-bank guide). A second night doubles those amp-hours, and lead-acid at about 50% depth of discharge doubles the nameplate again (Battle Born chemistry guide).

How to size it

This page uses 8 hours as the example input for one night. That duration is the assumption, not a Champion specification (Champion wattage chart).

  1. Read the watts from Champion: 30–60 W unheated, 60–120 W heated. No starting watts are listed (Champion wattage chart).
  2. Watt-hours are watts × hours. 30 × 8 = 240 Wh, 60 × 8 = 480 Wh, and 120 × 8 = 960 Wh (Battle Born battery-bank guide).
  3. Amp-hours at 12 V are watt-hours ÷ voltage. 240 ÷ 12 = 20 Ah, 480 ÷ 12 = 40 Ah, and 960 ÷ 12 = 80 Ah (Battle Born battery-bank guide).
  4. Two days off-grid doubles the capacity (Battle Born battery-bank guide). A second night takes the one-night figures to 40 Ah, 80 Ah, and 160 Ah.
  5. A 100 Ah lithium battery in the manual is 12 V, 100% usable, and rated 100 A continuous (Battle Born 100 Ah manual). An 80 Ah heated night fits in that one battery. Two heated nights at 160 Ah do not: 160 ÷ 100 = 1.6, so the count rounds up to 2 batteries (Battle Born battery-bank guide).
  6. Current is the easy part. 120 ÷ 12 = 10 A, far under 100 A continuous, so a CPAP will not trip that limit (Battle Born 100 Ah manual; Champion wattage chart).
  7. Lead-acid and AGM are typically limited to about 50% depth of discharge, and a 100 Ah lithium battery is compared with a 200 Ah lead-acid bank (Battle Born chemistry guide). One heated night of 80 Ah usable is about a 160 Ah lead-acid nameplate. Two heated nights, 160 Ah usable, are about a 320 Ah lead-acid nameplate.
  8. The manual also lists 3,000–5,000 deep discharge cycles for this lithium battery (Battle Born 100 Ah manual). That cycle range is why a CPAP load each night is usually argued on usable amp-hours, not on replacing the bank after one season.

A CPAP is often one line on a larger van list. The daily total belongs on the battery bank calculator.

Frequently asked questions

What size battery bank do I need for a CPAP?

For an 8-hour night, Champion's 30–60 W range is 20–40 Ah at 12 V, and the heated 60–120 W range is 40–80 Ah. Watt-hours are watts × 8, then divided by 12 volts (Champion wattage chart; Battle Born battery-bank guide).

What about two nights without charging?

Double the one-night bank. The Battle Born battery-bank guide says two days off-grid doubles capacity, so a 120 W heated night goes from 80 Ah to 160 Ah at 12 V.

Is one 100 Ah lithium battery enough?

For one heated night, yes: 80 Ah is under 100 Ah of 100% usable capacity. For two heated nights, 160 ÷ 100 = 1.6, so two batteries (Battle Born 100 Ah manual; Battle Born battery-bank guide).

Do I size lead-acid the same way?

Double the nameplate. About 50% depth of discharge means one heated night of 80 Ah usable is about 160 Ah of lead-acid, and two nights are about 320 Ah (Battle Born chemistry guide).
