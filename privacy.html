Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

Battery Bank Sizer (battery-bank-sizer.com) is a free static site owned by Jeremy Panasuk, New Auburn, Wisconsin. It is built to collect as little information as possible.

Information we collect

The sizing tables and formulas are static HTML. They run in your browser. This site has no account system and no form that sends loads, names, or email addresses to a server we operate. We do not collect names, emails, or calculator entries.

Scripts on this site

The home page and the topic map include a link to the printable amp-hour card and a link to TikTok @3jsa7 in the HTML. Those two pages also load one first-party script, assets/magnet-cta.js. The script adds those two links only if they are missing. It does not send data off this site, and it does not set cookies. Favicon and social-preview images are also served from this site.

Advertising

This site does not load Google AdSense or any other display-ad script.

Affiliate links

Some pages contain Amazon Associates links to Amazon.com. If you follow one and buy something, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. Those links do not change the sizing math. Amazon may set its own cookies after you leave this site for Amazon. Amazon's privacy policy covers that.

Analytics and cookies

We do not run our own analytics and we do not set our own tracking cookies. You can block or delete cookies in your browser.
