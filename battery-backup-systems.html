Battery Backup for a Fridge and a CPAP

Published October 3, 2026. Built by Jeremy Panasuk.

Direct answer: One outage day made from two published loads is 1,460 Wh. Battle Born’s deep-cycle example is a refrigerator at 100 W for 5 hours, which is 500 Wh. Champion’s CPAP chart tops the heated range at 120 W; the CPAP page already uses 8 hours for a night, and 120 × 8 = 960 Wh. 500 + 960 = 1,460 Wh. This site’s formula at 1 day, 12 V, and 0.80 depth of discharge is (1,460 × 1 × 1.25) ÷ 12 ÷ 0.80 = 190.1 Ah. Two 100 Ah lithium batteries are 200 Ah, which covers 190 Ah.

Eight hours is the duration the CPAP page uses. Champion publishes watts, not a night length. The 100 W and the 5 hours are Battle Born’s example, not a measurement from a fridge in this house. A sump pump is not folded into the 1,460 Wh. That load has its own running and starting watts on the sump-pump page.

Two 100 Ah batteries are 200 Ah and, by the parallel rule in the Battle Born manual, 200 A continuous. Nameplate energy is 200 × 12 = 2,400 Wh. Usable energy at the calculator’s 0.80 depth of discharge is 2,400 × 0.80 = 1,920 Wh. The formula’s stored need is 1,825 Wh, and 1,920 is above 1,825, which is why two batteries clear this particular day and one 100 Ah battery (1,200 × 0.80 = 960 Wh usable) does not.

Champion’s CPAP range is wider than the single 120 W used here: 30–60 W without the heater and 60–120 W heated. A 30 W night for 8 hours is 240 Wh, and the bank above is sized at the high heated end plus the fridge, not at 30 W. The fridge page also records Honda’s Energy Star running range and Honda’s 1,200 W start. Those starting watts are not added into the 1,460 Wh. A start is a surge the inverter has to cover. Treating 1,200 W as if it ran for 5 hours would invent 6,000 Wh the wattage chart does not describe.

What this backup is not

It is not a whole-house battery, and it is not the sump. Adding the pump means opening the sump page and adding that pump’s watt-hours to a new daily total, then rerunning the formula. A generator is the other tool when the bank will not carry the day. Wattage totals for that choice are on the generator sizing calculator. The lithium charge profile that refills the 200 Ah bank, including the 32°F charge cutoff, is on the lithium charger page.

Lead-acid and AGM at 0.50 depth of discharge change the same 1,460 Wh day: (1,460 × 1 × 1.25) ÷ 12 ÷ 0.50 = 304.2 Ah. That is the calculator’s DoD swap, which matches the chemistry guide’s “about 50%” limit only in the sense that this site uses 0.50 for that chemistry. The AGM guide keeps Lifeline’s own “twice the capacity” wording next to the 1.25 margin so the two are not silently merged.

A common mistake

Adding starting watts into the daily watt-hour budget. Honda’s refrigerator start is 1,200 W. Champion’s CPAP figure used here is a running range, not a start. The bank formula consumes watt-hours, which are running watts times hours. The inverter’s surge rating consumes the start. Counting 1,200 W × 5 hours as fridge energy, and then also counting 500 Wh, double-counts a number that was never energy. The other mistake is calling two 100 Ah batteries “enough backup” after a second appliance appears. The 190 Ah result is this pair of loads for one day. A second night doubles the day term: (1,460 × 2 × 1.25) ÷ 12 ÷ 0.80 = 380.2 Ah, which two batteries do not cover.

Limits, and when to call a pro

A transfer switch, a backed-up panel, and a standby generator interconnection are electrical work under the local code. This page does not size them. Medical equipment beyond the Champion wattage line — anything other than the published CPAP range — needs the device’s own power supply rating, not a substitution of 120 W. If the outage includes a sump in a flood, use the pump’s published watts and a generator plan; do not assume the 200 Ah fridge-and-CPAP bank also runs the pump. If you are inside a day and the battery has opened on low voltage, the Battle Born manual says many chargers must see more than 10 V and that a jump should happen within 24 hours of low-voltage disconnect or you risk damage and voiding the warranty.
