Best Battery for a Minn Kota Trolling Motor

"Best" here means Minn Kota's own good, better and best battery rows, not a brand pick.

Direct answer: Minn Kota's chart calls for a 12 V deep-cycle marine battery of at least 110 Ah for best results: group 24 (70 to 85 Ah) for good run time, group 27 (85 to 110 Ah) for better, group 31 (95 to 125 Ah) for best. Its 24 V motors, like the 80 lb, take two batteries in series; 36 V motors take three.

How to use it

  1. Find your motor's thrust and system voltage above. Single-motor pages: 30 lb, 55 lb, 80 lb, 112 lb.
  2. Buy one 12 V deep-cycle battery per 12 V of system voltage, all the same group and age, wired in series.
  3. Pick the group from Minn Kota's rows. Group 31 is its "best" row, and its 95 to 125 Ah range is where the 110 Ah recommendation sits.
  4. Estimate runtime: Ah ÷ amp draw. At full throttle that is the floor, not the typical day.

Frequently asked questions

What battery does Minn Kota recommend?

A 12 V deep-cycle marine battery, at least 110 Ah for best results. Minn Kota's chart rates group 24 (70 to 85 Ah) for good run time, group 27 (85 to 110 Ah) for better and group 31 (95 to 125 Ah) for best.

How many batteries does my Minn Kota need?

One per 12 V of system voltage. Minn Kota's chart lists 12 V, 24 V and 36 V motors, so that is one, two or three 12 V batteries in series. The 80 lb motor is 24 V, so two.

What breaker goes with it?

Minn Kota's chart lists a breaker for each motor, for example 60 A at 24 VDC for the 80 lb and 60 A at 36 VDC for the 112 lb.

How long will it run?

Divide battery amp-hours by amp draw. At the chart's max draw, a 110 Ah battery on a 50 A motor is 110 ÷ 50 = 2.2 hours. Max draw is intermittent, so most trips draw less and run longer.
