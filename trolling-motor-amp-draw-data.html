Trolling motor amp draw and battery sizing data

Minn Kota's charts list a 30 lb trolling motor at a 30 amp max draw on 12 volts, a 55 lb at 50 amps, an 80 lb at 56 amps on 24 volts and a 112 lb at 52 amps on 36 volts. Minn Kota says max draw is intermittent and recommends a deep-cycle battery of at least 110 Ah.

Last updated: October 10, 2026.

Built by Jeremy Panasuk.

Max amp draw is intermittent, as published by Minn Kota, not a continuous load rating. The last three rows are Minn Kota's recommended 12 V deep-cycle battery group sizes and amp-hour ranges for good, better and best run-time.
